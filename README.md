# Image converter for [Cosmoteer](https://store.steampowered.com/app/799600/)

No account needed or installation needed

Link to use : https://unhealthilyreliantonai.github.io/cosmoteer-image-converter/

### How to use :
1. Upload an image of your choice, you can browse from your files, drag & drop, or paste with Ctrl + V
2. Tweak the settings as you like
3. Click convert, a preview will appear, showing how the shape will look in-game
4. Click the export button to download the .ship.png file
5. Put the file in your Saved Ships folder, or drag & drop it to the in-game editor directly

On windows, saved ships should be located at :
> %userprofile%/Saved Games/Cosmoteer/[steam ID]/Saved Ships

### Things to know :
* Everything is processed locally, your images aren't uploaded in any server
* Black shapes on white/transparent backgrounds work best
* Smoothing, symmetry and unification can take longer to process, depending on your settings and your computer
* Unification can fail for very fragmented images
* The tool generates armor-based silhouettes, not equipped nor functional ships
* Unofficial community tool, unaffiliated with Walternate Realities
